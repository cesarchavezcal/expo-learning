# Proposal: E-Ink Ebook Reader for Expo Learning

## Problem Statement
Mobile reading applications suffer from cognitive clutter, glossy UI elements, distracting interactions, and inadequate typography scaling that fatigues readers.

## Proposed Solution
An immersive, high-contrast, distraction-free E-Ink ebook reader built on Expo Router and Scandinavian design principles.

### Key Deliverables
1. **Curated E-Ink Library**: Bookshelf showing classic literature with progress tracking.
2. **E-Ink Reader Engine**: Paged chapter renderer with custom typography controls, margin sizing, and simulated e-ink waveform refresh.
3. **Table of Contents & Quick Navigation**: Frictionless chapter jumping and bookmarking.
4. **Persistent State**: Local storage preserving reading position and user preferences across sessions.
